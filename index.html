Server Rules

ច្បាប់ និងបទបញ្ជាក្នុងការលេងនៅលើ Server Kachy MC។ សូមអាននិងគោរពតាមដើម្បីបរិយាកាសលេងដ៏ល្អសម្រាប់ទាំងអស់គ្នា!

01

NO HACKING OR EXPLOITING

ហាមប្រើប្រាស់កម្មវិធីជំនួយ (Hacks/Cheats) ឬកេងចំណេញពីកំហុសហ្គេម (Bugs)

The use of modded clients (X-Ray, KillAura, Fly hacks, etc.), auto-clickers, or exploiting server bugs to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. Violations will result in an immediate permanent ban without warning.

02

BE RESPECTFUL (NO TOXICITY)

គោរពអ្នកដទៃ ហាមប្រើពាក្យជេរប្រមាថ ឬរើសអើង

Treat all players and staff with respect. Hate speech, racism, severe toxicity, death threats, and harassment are not allowed in public chat or private messages. Keep the environment welcoming for everyone.

03

NO GRIEFING OR STEALING

ហាមលួចអីវ៉ាន់ ឬបំផ្លាញផ្ទះ/សំណង់អ្នកដទៃ

Unless you are explicitly playing in a designated Anarchy/Faction zone, destroying other players' builds or stealing their items from unprotected chests is a bannable offense. Protect your land, but respect others' properties.

04

NO SPAMMING OR ADVERTISING

ហាមស្ពែមសារ ឬផ្សព្វផ្សាយ Server ដទៃ

Do not flood the chat with repeated messages, excessive capital letters, or meaningless symbols. Advertising other Minecraft servers, Discord links, or outside products will result in a mute or ban.

Consequences & Appeals

By joining Kachy MC, you automatically agree to abide by these rules. Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse. If you believe you were falsely punished, you may open an appeal ticket in our Discord Server.
